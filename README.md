## Projects

### Final Project — Telecom Operations, A/B Testing & SQL Analysis
> Three-case capstone project combining operational analysis, experimental design, and SQL querying across independent business scenarios.

| Case | Domain | Key Techniques |
|---|---|---|
| Case 1: Operator Performance | Telecom (CallMeMaybe) | KPI design, Mann-Whitney U, Ranking |
| Case 2: A/B Test Evaluation | E-commerce | Funnel analysis, Z-test, Experiment integrity |
| Case 3: SQL Book Startup | Publishing / Startups | SQL queries, Relational DB analysis |

**Case 1** Built an operator classification system using three KPIs (missed call rate, wait time, outbound volume). Validated differences with Mann-Whitney U tests and delivered ranked inefficiency results with monitoring and coaching recommendations.

**Case 2** Evaluated an A/B test on a recommendation system. The valid sample dropped from 6,000 to 2,594 due to contamination. A concurrent marketing campaign acted as a confounding variable. Group B underperformed control across all funnel stages. Recommendation: invalidate and redesign the experiment.

**Case 3** Answered five business questions for a book startup using SQL: 819 post-2000 titles in catalog, Penguin Books as top publisher partner, J.K. Rowling as the ideal onboarding author (4.29/5 avg rating), and power users averaging 24 text reviews each.

### Sprint 6 — Global Video Game Sales Analysis

Ice, a global game retailer, needed to identify which platforms, genres, and ESRB ratings drove sales to plan their 2017 campaign. I analyzed historical data across North America, Europe, and Japan using EDA and hypothesis testing. PS4 leads in Western markets while Japan favors portable consoles. Action and Shooter genres dominate in NA and EU, while Role-Playing is far more popular in Japan. Regional segmentation is essential for effective campaign planning.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · Seaborn · SciPy

---

### Sprint 5 — Telecom Prepaid Plan Revenue Analysis

Megaline needed to know which of their two prepaid plans (Surf and Ultimate) generated more revenue to allocate their advertising budget. I analyzed usage data from 500 customers across calls, SMS, and internet, merging datasets and applying hypothesis testing. Surf users operate near their monthly limits and generate overage charges. SMS usage is low across both plans, pointing to a margin improvement opportunity.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · NumPy · SciPy · Statistical Hypothesis Testing

---

### Sprint 4 — Instacart Grocery Shopping Behavior

Using a modified version of Instacart's real dataset, I performed end-to-end EDA to uncover purchasing patterns. After cleaning nulls and duplicates, I analyzed reorder rates, order timing, product popularity, and cart composition. Fresh and organic staples dominate purchases, most users show strong product loyalty, and purchasing behavior follows clear time-based patterns — all directly actionable for inventory and promotional strategy.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · EDA · Data Cleaning

---

### Sprint 3 — Music Streaming Behavior Analysis

Real streaming data was used to test whether music listening patterns differ between two cities (Springfield and Shelbyville) across days of the week. I cleaned the dataset, handled missing values, and applied hypothesis testing. While overall activity levels differ between cities, the weekly pattern structure is similar in both — suggesting time-based targeting outperforms city-based segmentation.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · Hypothesis Testing · EDA
